Othello board. Use the arrow keys to move one square. Hold two arrow keys together to move diagonally. Press N or right bracket for the next legal move, and P or left bracket for the previous legal move. Press Enter or Space to play on a legal empty square. Each square tells you whether it contains a black disc, a white disc, or is empty, and whether an empty square is a legal move for you.
